SynthID is applied by Google to content from its own generative AI products. Here is where you're most likely to run into it.

## Images

- **Gemini app**: images created or edited with Gemini.
- **Imagen**: Google's image model, including through Vertex AI.
- **Google Photos**: some AI edits, such as Magic Editor, add SynthID.

## Video

- **Veo**: Google's video generation model, including in Gemini and Flow.
- **YouTube Shorts**: videos made with built-in Veo features.

## Audio and music

- **Lyria**: Google's music generation model.
- **NotebookLM**: AI-generated audio overviews.

## Text

- **Gemini**: text responses can carry a SynthID Text watermark.
- **Open-source models**: developers can add SynthID Text to their own models using Google's open-source release.

## How to check a file

Google lets you check for SynthID with its SynthID Detector, and you can ask Gemini whether an image was made with Google AI.

Read more in [What is SynthID?](/what-is-synthid/) or the [how to remove SynthID](/how-to-remove-synthid/) guide.
